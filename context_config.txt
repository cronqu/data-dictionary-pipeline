STUDY_NAME: Wound Infection Prediction Study

STUDY_CONTEXT:
This dataset is from a wound infection prediction machine learning study
conducted using Vancouver Coastal Health (VCH) community home health data
(2019–2021). Data come from two source systems: PARIS (Patient Assessment
and Review Information System), a community care electronic medical record
used by VCH, and Pixalere, a wound care documentation platform. The study
aims to predict wound infection indicators and antimicrobial product use
from patient demographic, clinical, and social determinants of health data.

DATA_SOURCES:
- PARIS: Community health EMR (tables A01–B20 as described in the PARIS
  data requirements document). Covers demographics, assessments, referrals,
  immunizations, clinical care plans, and sociodemographic variables.
- Pixalere: Wound care platform (ax_structured = wound assessments per visit;
  wp_structured = wound profile/etiology/goal-of-care table). Covers wound
  characteristics, product use, and care plans.

TARGET_VARIABLE:
The 'label' column is a binary outcome (True/False) indicating whether the
patient received antimicrobial wound products (e.g., acticoat, calcicare)
as a proxy for nurse-directed wound infection prophylaxis.
